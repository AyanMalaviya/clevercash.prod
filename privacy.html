Privacy Policy for CleverCash 1. Introduction CleverCash ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use our mobile application. 2. Data Collection and Storage CleverCash is a privacy-first application. All your financial data, accounts, and transactions are stored entirely locally on your device's internal storage. We do not operate any database servers, and we do not collect, harvest, or sell your personal financial information. 3. Google Drive Integration (Cloud Backups) CleverCash offers an optional cloud backup feature using Google Drive. If enabled, the app requests restricted access to your Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file) solely to upload and download your backup files. Zero-Knowledge Encryption: Before any data leaves your device, it is heavily encrypted using a custom password you provide. We do not know this password, and Google cannot read your financial data. CleverCash can only access the specific backup files it creates. It cannot read, view, or modify your personal photos, documents, or emails. 4. Data Deletion Because your data is stored locally, you can permanently delete all your data at any time by utilizing the "Hard Reset" feature within the app's settings or by uninstalling the application. You may also delete your encrypted cloud backups directly from the app settings. 5. Contact Us If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: ayanmalaviya111@gmail.com
